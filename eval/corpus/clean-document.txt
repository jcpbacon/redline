CREATOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Creator Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on September 22, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Fernhill Outdoor Supply LLC, an Oregon limited liability company at 41 Alder Lane, Portland, Oregon 97205 ("Client"), and the content creator named in the signature block below ("Creator"). Client and Creator are each a "Party" and together the "Parties." The Parties negotiated these terms and each had the opportunity to review them with an advisor before signing.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Project" means the promotion of Client's Trailhead day-pack range described in Schedule A.

1.2 "Deliverables" means the specific videos and posts listed in Schedule A.

1.3 "Content" means the final, published versions of the Deliverables. Drafts, outtakes and unused footage are not Content.

1.4 "Fee" means the amount stated in Schedule A.

2. SERVICES

2.1 Creator will produce the Deliverables described in Schedule A and publish them on Creator's YouTube and Instagram accounts on the dates listed there, or on other dates the Parties agree in writing.

2.2 Creator will share each Deliverable with Client at least four (4) business days before publication. Client may give written comments within two (2) business days. Creator will make one (1) round of reasonable revisions that respond to those comments. A Deliverable is treated as approved if Client does not comment within that period.

2.3 Creator decides the creative approach, tone and editing of each Deliverable, consistent with the talking points in Schedule A.

2.4 Creator will label every Deliverable as sponsored content, as required by the Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides.

2.5 Changes to the Deliverables or the schedule take effect only when written and signed by both Parties. If a change adds work, the Parties will agree on an additional fee in the same writing.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Client will pay Creator the Fee in two instalments: fifty percent (50%) within five (5) business days after the Effective Date, and the remaining fifty percent (50%) within fifteen (15) days after the last Deliverable is published.

3.2 Creator will send an invoice for each instalment. Invoices are payable by bank transfer.

3.3 Any amount not paid when due accrues a late fee of one and a half percent (1.5%) per month until paid. Creator may pause work on any unpublished Deliverable while an undisputed invoice is more than fifteen (15) days overdue.

3.4 Client will reimburse reasonable production expenses that Client approves in writing in advance, within fifteen (15) days of receiving receipts.

4. OWNERSHIP AND LICENCE

4.1 Creator owns all Content and all drafts, footage and other materials Creator creates for the Project.

4.2 Creator grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to share, embed and repost the Content on Client's own website and organic social media accounts for twelve (12) months from each Deliverable's publication date.

4.3 Paid advertising use of the Content, use beyond the twelve-month period, or use of Creator's name or likeness outside the Content requires a separate written agreement and fee.

4.4 Client keeps ownership of its trademarks, product images and other materials it supplies, and grants Creator a limited licence to use them only in the Deliverables.

4.5 After the licence period ends, Client is not required to remove posts already shared, but will not create new uses of the Content.

5. CREATOR'S OTHER WORK

5.1 For thirty (30) days on either side of each Deliverable's publication date, Creator will not publish paid content for the day-pack products of the companies listed in Schedule B.

5.2 Nothing in this Agreement restricts Creator from working with any other company in any other product category, during or after the term, or from continuing any agreement Creator disclosed to Client before signing.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and ends when the last Deliverable has been published and paid for, unless terminated earlier under this Section 6. It does not renew unless both Parties sign a new agreement.

6.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within ten (10) business days after receiving written notice describing it.

6.3 Either Party may end this Agreement for any reason on fourteen (14) days' written notice. If Client ends the Agreement under this Section 6.3, Client will pay for every Deliverable completed or in progress at the date of notice, plus twenty-five percent (25%) of the remaining Fee as a cancellation fee.

6.4 If either Party publicly makes statements that are hateful or harassing, the other Party may terminate on written notice. Fees for Deliverables already published remain payable and are not refundable.

7. WARRANTIES

7.1 Creator warrants that the Content will be Creator's original work, except for materials Client supplies or that Client approves in writing.

7.2 Client warrants that all product claims and information it gives Creator are accurate and that Client has the right to provide the materials it supplies.

7.3 Each Party will comply with the laws that apply to its own activities under this Agreement.

8. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY

8.1 Each Party will defend the other against any third-party claim to the extent it arises from the first Party's breach of its warranties in Section 7, and will pay any resulting damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

8.2 Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental or consequential damages.

8.3 Except for amounts owed under Section 3, each Party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the Fee.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party will keep confidential any non-public business information the other Party shares under this Agreement and will use it only to perform this Agreement.

9.2 Creator may disclose that Creator has a paid relationship with Client, and either Party may disclose this Agreement to its lawyers, accountants or as required by law.

10. GENERAL

10.1 Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.

10.2 Disputes. The Parties will first try to resolve any dispute by good-faith discussion for at least fifteen (15) days. If that does not resolve it, either Party may bring a claim in the courts of Multnomah County, Oregon, or in small claims court where the claim qualifies.

10.3 Notices. Notices must be in writing and may be sent by email to the address each Party lists in the signature block. A notice is received one business day after it is sent.

10.4 Independent contractor. Creator is an independent contractor and controls how, when and where the work is done.

10.5 Assignment. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

10.6 Entire agreement. This Agreement, including its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the Parties about the Project. It can be changed only in a writing signed by both Parties.

10.7 Severability. If a court finds any part of this Agreement unenforceable, the rest of it remains in effect.

SCHEDULE A

Deliverables:
(a) One (1) YouTube video of 8 to 12 minutes reviewing the Trailhead 22L day pack on a day hike, to be published on October 9, 2026.
(b) Two (2) Instagram Reels of 30 to 60 seconds each, to be published on October 14, 2026 and October 21, 2026.

Talking points: pack weight, the hip-belt pockets, and the recycled-nylon shell.

Fee: Five thousand two hundred US dollars (USD $5,200).
