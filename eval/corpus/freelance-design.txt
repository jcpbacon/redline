WEBSITE DESIGN AGREEMENT

This Website Design Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on August 4, 2026 between Pellar Home Goods Ltd. ("Client") and the freelance designer named below ("Designer").

1. The Work

1.1 Designer will design a five-page marketing website for Client, as described in the attached Scope of Work (the "Work Product").

1.2 Client may request up to three (3) rounds of revisions to each page. Further revisions are billed at Designer's hourly rate of ninety US dollars (USD $90).

2. Payment

2.1 Designer will invoice Client for the fixed price of six thousand US dollars (USD $6,000) when the Work Product is delivered.

2.2 Client will pay each invoice within one hundred and twenty (120) days of receipt, and Client may set off against any invoice any amount Client believes Designer owes it.

3. Ownership

3.1 Designer assigns to Client all rights in the Work Product, including any tools, templates, code libraries and design systems Designer used to create it, whether made before or during this Agreement.

3.2 Designer may show screenshots of the finished website in Designer's portfolio.

4. Defects

4.1 Designer will fix any error in the Work Product that Client reports within thirty (30) days of delivery, at no charge.

4.2 Designer will reimburse Client for any losses Client suffers as a result of a defect in the Work Product, with no limit on the amount.

5. Ending the Agreement

5.1 Either Party may end this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice. Client will pay for work completed up to the date the notice takes effect.

6. General

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

6.2 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties about the Work Product.
