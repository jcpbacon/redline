BRAND PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Brand Partnership Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northwick Wellness Co., a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 220 Harbor Street, Suite 900, Boston, Massachusetts 02110 ("Brand"), and the individual content creator identified in the signature block below ("Creator"). Brand and Creator are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

Brand offers this Agreement on a standard form to all creators participating in its Autumn Glow campaign. By signing below, Creator accepts these terms in full.

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Campaign" means Brand's Autumn Glow marketing campaign for its Dew Serum product line, as further described in the Campaign Brief.

1.2 "Campaign Brief" means the written document supplied by Brand describing the Deliverables, posting schedule, talking points, mandatory disclosures and visual guidelines for the Campaign, as updated from time to time.

1.3 "Content" means all photographs, videos, captions, audio, livestreams, stories and other materials created by or for Creator in connection with the Campaign, including drafts, outtakes and unused footage.

1.4 "Deliverables" means the Content items listed in Schedule A that Creator must produce and publish under this Agreement.

1.5 "Fee" means the total compensation stated in Schedule A.

2. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES

2.1 Creator will produce the Deliverables in accordance with the Campaign Brief and will publish each Deliverable on Creator's Instagram and TikTok accounts on the dates set out in Schedule A.

2.2 Creator will submit each Deliverable to Brand for review no later than five (5) business days before its scheduled publication date. Brand may request reasonable revisions, and Creator will make up to two (2) rounds of revisions per Deliverable at no additional charge.

2.3 Creator will include all disclosures required by the Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides, including the label "#ad" or "Paid partnership with Northwick," in a clear and conspicuous position on every Deliverable.

2.4 Creator will keep each published Deliverable live on Creator's accounts for at least ninety (90) days after publication and will not edit or archive it during that period without Brand's prior written consent.

2.5 Brand may amend the Campaign Brief or any term of this Agreement at any time by sending notice to Creator, and Creator's continued performance after that notice constitutes acceptance of the amended terms.

3. COMPENSATION

3.1 In full consideration for the Deliverables and the rights granted in this Agreement, Brand will pay Creator the Fee.

3.2 Brand will pay the Fee within ninety (90) days after Brand's written acceptance of all Deliverables, and Brand may withhold acceptance in its sole discretion.

3.3 Creator is responsible for all taxes on amounts paid under this Agreement. Creator will provide a completed Form W-9 before any payment is made.

3.4 Brand will reimburse pre-approved, documented out-of-pocket production expenses up to a total of two hundred dollars ($200), payable together with the Fee.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 Creator hereby assigns to Brand all right, title and interest in and to the Content, and grants Brand a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use Creator’s name, likeness, voice and biography in connection with the Content in any media now known or later developed.

4.2 Creator waives any moral rights Creator may have in the Content to the extent permitted by law.

4.3 Creator represents that the Content will be original to Creator and will not include music, footage or other third-party material unless Brand has approved its use in writing.

5. EXCLUSIVITY

5.1 During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Creator shall not create, publish or promote content for any company that Brand considers to be a competitor in the beauty, wellness or lifestyle categories.

5.2 Creator will disclose to Brand, before signing, any existing agreement with a skincare company that remains in effect on the Effective Date.

6. TERM, RENEWAL AND TERMINATION

6.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for six (6) months (the "Term").

6.2 This Agreement will automatically renew for successive six (6) month terms unless Creator gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

6.3 Brand may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, on seven (7) days' written notice, and in that event no portion of the Fee shall be owed for Deliverables not yet accepted.

6.4 Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after receiving notice describing it.

6.5 Sections 4, 5, 7, 8 and 10 survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement.

7. CONDUCT

7.1 Creator will comply with all applicable laws and with the community guidelines of each platform on which a Deliverable is published.

7.2 If Creator engages in any conduct that, in Brand’s sole judgment, brings Creator or Brand into public disrepute — whether or not related to the Campaign — Brand may terminate this Agreement immediately and Creator shall repay all Fees previously received.

7.3 Creator will not make any statement about the Dew Serum product line that is not supported by the claims listed in the Campaign Brief.

8. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

8.1 Creator shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Brand and its affiliates, officers, employees and agents from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to the Content or this Agreement, without limitation as to amount.

8.2 In no event will Brand be liable to Creator for any indirect, incidental, consequential or punitive damages, and Brand's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the portion of the Fee actually paid to Creator.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Creator will keep confidential the terms of this Agreement, the Campaign Brief, and any non-public information about Brand's products, pricing or launch plans that Creator receives in connection with the Campaign.

9.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of Creator, or that Creator is required by law to disclose.

10. GENERAL

10.1 Governing law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Province of Manitoba, Canada, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

10.2 Notices. All notices to Brand must be sent by registered post to Brand's legal department at Unit 4, 1180 Industrial Parkway, Winnipeg, Manitoba R3H 0Y2, Canada.

10.3 Independent contractor. Creator is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties.

10.4 Assignment. Creator may not assign or subcontract this Agreement or any of Creator's obligations under it without Brand's prior written consent. Brand may assign this Agreement to any affiliate or successor without notice to Creator.

10.5 Entire agreement. This Agreement, including Schedule A and the Campaign Brief, is the entire agreement between the Parties about its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, proposals and understandings.

10.6 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.

10.7 Counterparts. This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
